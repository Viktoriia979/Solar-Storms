Solar Storms

Solar storms are powerful bursts of energy and charged particles released from the Sun. They can happen because of solar flares and coronal mass ejections (CMEs). When these particles travel through space and reach Earth, they can interact with our planet’s magnetic field and atmosphere.

Solar storms form when the Sun’s magnetic fields become twisted and unstable. When these magnetic fields suddenly change or reconnect, they can release huge amounts of energy and send charged particles into space. If these particles are travelling towards Earth, they can cause a solar storm when they reach our planet.

Solar storms can cause problems for technology on Earth and in space. Strong solar storms can interfere with satellites, GPS, radio communication and electricity networks. They can also affect astronauts because of increased radiation. However, solar storms can create beautiful auroras, such as the Northern Lights and Southern Lights.
